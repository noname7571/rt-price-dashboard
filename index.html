Reading the Chart

This dashboard shows live cryptocurrency prices. Here's what everything means:

Trading Pair

BTC/USDT means "the price of 1 Bitcoin in USDT" — a digital dollar that always equals ~$1. The left coin is what you're buying, the right is what you pay with.

▲

Green Candle — Price Up

The price closed higher than it opened. Bottom of body = open price. Top of body = close price. A bullish period.

▼

Red Candle — Price Down

The price closed lower than it opened. Top of body = open price. Bottom of body = close price. A bearish period.

╿

Wicks (Thin Lines)

The thin lines above and below the candle body show the highest and lowest prices reached during that period — even if only briefly.

Volume Bars (Bottom Chart)

Each bar shows how much of the coin was traded in that period. Tall bar on a big price move = strong conviction. Low volume = quiet market.

%

24h Change / High / Low

+3.5% means the price is 3.5% higher than 24 hours ago. 24h High/Low are the highest and lowest prices reached in the past 24 hours.
